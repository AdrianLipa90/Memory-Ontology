## Proposed reversible extension

For x=q(n) in [0,1) and history coordinate y in [0,1), set b=floor(2x).
\[
B(x,y)=(2x-b,(y+b)/2).
\]
On each branch the Jacobian is diag(2,1/2), determinant 1. This is the standard invertible baker map on the half-open square (branch boundaries assigned by floor). Its inverse is
\[
b=\lfloor2y'\rfloor,\qquad x=(x'+b)/2,\qquad y=2y'-b.
\]
Starting y=0, after k steps y stores the reversed consumed parity prefix as a dyadic rational. Retain exact integer state and horizon/lineage; finite floating-point storage eventually loses old bits. The 2D extension is not a proof of an area-preserving full HTRI evolution. Phase doubling is locally expanding in FS equatorial distance; its terminal 3-cycle has multiplier 8 on the ambient circle, not an attracting continuous orbit. Full reversible map has no volume-contracting attracting basin on the square. Arithmetic finite terminal recurrence does not imply spontaneous metastability or semantic consolidation.

For finite uniform seeds 1..4096 and H=32 unaccelerated steps, exact rational/BigInt witness checks phase shift, SOH branch conjugacy, accelerated odd z^4, and reversal of arithmetic state, rational phase and history coordinate. Endpoints alone have 719 distinct values, largest fibre 301. Conditional entropy H(N0|N32)=4.3331005659861335 bits, H(N32)=7.6668994340138665 bits. This is ensemble-specific uncertainty in the missing initial address, not energy, semantic mass or a minimal universal storage bound. A tagged bit per unaccelerated step is sufficient but need not be optimal. All 4096 tested paths hit 1 within cap 10000, max first-hit 237; no general convergence inference.

## Memory interpretation and next experiment

Projection onto x discards history; reinserting y recovers it exactly in the mathematical model. The history coordinate by itself is passive in this map: it does NOT affect x'. It is a retained record, not yet causal memory under the core law.
