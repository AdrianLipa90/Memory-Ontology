# Epistemic debt and dependency-aware repair — v0.1

Author direction: Adrian Lipa, 2026-11-26. Source conceptual basis: The Reality of the Imaginary V32, printed pp.274–284, especially pp.279–283; [truth consolidation](truth-dependency-consolidation-v0.1.md), [archive checkpoint restore](archive-checkpoint-restore-v0.2.md). Candidate research extension; not a universal cost law.

## Distinctions

Unknown or unresolved relation is not a false established relation. Honest HOLD retains the potential of the paired branches (-i,+i); an epistemic decision policy must preserve the reason for unresolved status. A factual error need not imply deceptive intent. False claims about knowledge or ignorance, deliberate manipulation of a reference, and loss of decisive distinctions in a summary can introduce epistemic debt. Intent cannot be inferred from a graph discrepancy alone. Distinguish actual missing information, failed retrieval, insufficient evidence, and an unexamined source. A system must not report a search as completed when it was not. A negative finding is scoped to sources and query actually examined.

## Repair obligation

Let D be a directed typed dependency graph with edges from premise/observation to dependent claim or transformation. For a corrected seed set S, let R(S) be its reflexive downstream reachable set. Candidate revalidation workload C(S)=sum_{v in R(S)} c_v for fixed nonnegative per-node costs and one revalidation per unique node. This is a model of review workload, not an estimate of real harm or guaranteed repair cost. Reachability marks candidates to re-evaluate, not automatic falsity. An alternate independent support may preserve a conclusion. Cycles, repeated recomputation, external effects and context/time-scope changes need separate costs and semantics. For fixed graph and weights, C(S union T)=C(S)+C(T)-sum_{v in R(S) intersect R(T)}c_v, so overlapping debts are not independently additive.

For complete binary tree depth n, unit cost and a root correction, T_n=2^(n+1)-1. For width-two fully connected adjacent layers with a single root, U_n=1+2n even though both have 2^n root-to-layer-n paths. At depth eight the counts are 511 and 17, respectively, with 256 paths each. The exponential case needs non-reconvergent branching or a separate path-sensitive workload. A finite graph's unique-node workload cannot exceed its total weight. Honest uncertainty does not have universally constant or zero cost: an investigation can be expensive and unresolved premises may have explicitly conditional descendants. It avoids an unjustified promotion to established fact, not all future work.

## Contraction of facts

Let pi project detailed evidence/history to a summary. For an admitted future question family and correction operator F_e, require O=O_bar pi and pi F_e=F_bar_e pi where defined. If two distinct evidence states x,y have pi(x)=pi(y) but O(x) differs from O(y), no deterministic readout O_bar of that summary alone can preserve both answers. The same argument applies to future evidence/correction events for which projected updates differ. This is an elementary factorization obstruction. V32's example of three copies versus three independent reports is a concrete counterexample: retaining only transmission count merges two states with different posterior odds. Compression can omit details safely only relative to the declared observation/update contracts. Unknown origin remains unknown; a plausible reconstruction must not be entered as recovered provenance. Deliberate omission and ordinary lossy compression are distinct; the mathematical obstruction does not diagnose intent.
