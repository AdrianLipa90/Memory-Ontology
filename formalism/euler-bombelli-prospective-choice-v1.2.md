# EBRE prospective choice and character — v1.2 candidate

Author research direction: Adrian Lipa. Base 273b7bd.
Extends [EBRE correction](euler-bombelli-dependency-repair-v1.1.md),
[prospective memory](prospective-memory-intention-will-sacrifice-v0.1.md),
[determination](prospective-memory-determination-v0.2.md) and
[Character Depository](../projects/pip-project/PIP_CHARACTER_DEPOSIT_STEP_V0_1.md).

Intention times time = will; will times determination = sacrifice.
Keep current bounded commitment, historical intention exposure, goal,
route availability, choice, outcome, affect, and proof distinct.
The original general prospective PNV transforms remain unbound.
Here a finite callback-free specialization evaluates I=1, duration=2,
four comparable prior allocation opportunities and beta(1,1).
D=(accepted+1)/6, W=2, P=2*(accepted+1)/6 (exact integer pairs).
The historical opportunities are selected by the fixture, not inferred
from arbitrary Hotel events. Their observed allocations are independent
of the theorem proof. Fixed cost comparison 1, detour cost 2, hard budget
2, cost scale 1: accept detour iff P>=1 (accepted>=2). Open route accepts;
unknown route HOLD; verified withdrawal REVISE regardless of readiness.
These are explicit model choices, not a general calibrated law.

The finite PNV program computes the proposal vector [GO,HOLD,REVISE],
retaining original selected EBRE proof status as context; that status
does not enter the proposal. Conversely the policy does not change proof
validity. GO is a proposal to examine a route, never a claim of arrival.
Source preconditions: exact integer counts in 0..4, sum 4, valid route
status 0..3, independent observed history ref. N_OK is a diagnostic,
not a new runtime gate or general validation. No new callback or opcode.

Use the existing Character Depository separately on actual selected
synthetic allocation observations and a comparable binary GO/HOLD
choice observation; no withdrawal or unknown opportunity enters the
comparable denominator. Historical determination is a beta posterior
mean, not the character vector. No rewrite of history after a proposal.
Original Hotel operators assign occurrence indices, not proof values.

Test all 5 counts x 4 route states x 2 proof results, matched-history
intervention, truth independence, withdrawal/unknown controls, exact
character counts, and actual TCD registry card references. TCD view
composition remains observational; no invented affect-to-policy
constitutive map or term IDs. The actual native scheduler, prospective
transform registration, live PNAML transport and v0.9 cross-repo CI
remain open. No new runtime, memory store or plugin release.
