# Epistemic correction and recurrence — v0.2

Author research direction: Adrian Lipa, 2026-11-26. Extends [epistemic debt v0.1](epistemic-debt-dependency-repair-v0.1.md) and [truth dependency consolidation](truth-dependency-consolidation-v0.1.md). Candidate contract, not a new permission boundary or a modification of canonical HOLD.

## Preserve distinct mechanisms

Maintain distinct typed indicators for (1) declaration of ignorance incompatible with verified available knowledge of the same scoped claim; (2) a known false proposition asserted as established fact; (3) a material undisclosed contextual reframe; (4) contraction of decisive distinctions in a summary; (5) recurrence of a previously corrected mechanism; (6) claiming execution absent execution. The third is a discrepancy indicator, not proof of deliberate manipulation. Likewise an observed discrepancy is not sufficient to establish deceptive intent. Ordinary error, inaccessible information, failed retrieval, unexamined sources and genuine uncertainty remain distinguishable. A false execution claim is not necessarily intentional. No single debt score replaces the typed vector or its source lineage.

## Correction as a memory event

Proposed record C=(occurrence_id, scope, original_action_ref, mechanism_id, violated_contract, evidence_ref, acknowledged_at, revised_action_contract, priority, verification_refs). The original error remains in its historical context; the correction is a new event. A correction that concerns recurrence has high consolidation priority within the proposed research memory profile. This is a requested priority rule, not a claim of persistence in an actual agent. Acknowledgement is not a behavioral witness. Forgiveness does not establish that a repair was executed or eliminate the need to preserve the correction. Nor does it create a numerical debt or obligation on the person who forgives.

For a later occurrence, recurrence is the conjunction of an admitted previous correction, equivalence of the relevant failure mechanism under a declared scope, and violation of the corrected contract. Exact identifier equality in the finite fixture is only a special case; real semantic equivalence needs admitted typed evidence and cannot be inferred from wording alone. A newly encountered error, an unrelated mechanism, or a valid change of context is not automatically recurrence.

For a matched test of a correction, compare subsequent actions under relevant equivalent conditions before and after the corrective event. Require preservation of decisive distinctions, truthful status reporting, and provenance, without silently changing the goal or scope. A declaration of improvement does not establish changed behavior. Any failed repeat test remains evidence and should be acknowledged with its correction. No assumption that mere absence of a repeat over a finite test proves future reliability.

## Consolidation

A projection must preserve the distinctions required to identify a corrected mechanism, the revised contract and the evidence needed to evaluate later recurrence. Retain occurrence lineage separately from a compact mechanism profile. If different histories yield the same summary but differ on an admitted observation or correction, that summary cannot answer both faithfully. Ordinary compression and deliberate selective omission are distinct. Preserve unknowns explicitly. A correction of a factual claim and a gradual change to learned dispositions remain separate, as in V32 Chapter 31.
